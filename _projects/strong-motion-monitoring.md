## Strong-motion records

I work on strong-motion databases and ground-motion prediction equations, with current studies involving seismic records from Iran and the Yunnan region of China.

## Field measurements

My experimental work includes structural health monitoring, management and analysis of continuous seismic records, field measurements of buildings and ground vibrations, and maintenance of seismic instrumentation and monitoring systems.

## Research context

These activities complement my work on [seismic wave propagation and soil–structure interaction]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}), connecting numerical models with recorded earthquake response.
