## Research question

How do nonlinear soil behavior and deformable foundations affect structural response under large, near-fault earthquake motions?

## Approach

My doctoral work uses finite-element and finite-difference methods to examine coupled soil–foundation–structure systems. I investigate the effects of soil nonlinearity, foundation flexibility, wave incidence, and near-fault pulse-like motions.

I also develop computational tools for generating seismic input motions, including plane waves, surface waves, and near-field motions for numerical models.
