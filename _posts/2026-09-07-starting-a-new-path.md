For a long time, I've wanted a place to write: a small personal corner of the internet for my thoughts, ideas, and experiences.

My name is Hossein, and more than anything, I see myself as an explorer. I like searching, experimenting, breaking things, rebuilding them, and seeing how far I can go with the knowledge I have and the tools around me.

My interest in research, programming, technology, and building useful things isn't limited to work or university. It naturally finds its way into my everyday life. Sometimes, a small idea or a simple need makes me stop and wonder: *Is there a better way to do this? Can I make it simpler, faster, or more useful?* Those small questions are often where things begin.

I enjoy looking at a problem and trying to solve it with whatever I have available. Sometimes that means writing some code. Sometimes it means experimenting with a new technology. Other times, it means building something from scratch simply because I want to understand how it works. And that's a big part of what I want this blog to be about.

This isn't meant to be an academic or formal space. I already spend enough time around papers, and writing (or reading) another one isn't exactly what I want to do in my free time. Here, I want to write the way I'd tell a story to a friend.

I want to share what I'm working on, what I'm experimenting with, the ideas that randomly appear in my head, the things I build for fun, and sometimes even the things that don't work. Some ideas might become real projects. Some might stay small. Others might never be finished at all. That's okay.

For me, the interesting part is the process: thinking, searching, trying, failing, learning, and trying again.

Maybe one of the reasons I finally decided to start this blog is that I've been living alone. When you spend more time with yourself, some thoughts become a little louder. Writing feels like a way to hold on to those thoughts and experiences instead of letting them simply disappear with time. Even if nobody reads this, I still want to write.

I want these posts to remain as a small trace of my life, something I can return to years from now and remember what I was thinking about, what I was building, what I was struggling with, and what excited me at that point in my life.

So this blog will probably become a mixture of my personal and professional life, because for me, those two worlds are often connected. Research, programming, technology, everyday problems, random ideas, experiments, and the things I build in my free time all tend to overlap.

I also want writing here to become a habit. For now, I'm setting myself a simple goal: at least one post every month. Eventually, I'd love to make it a weekly habit and regularly share what I've been doing, learning, or building. We'll see where it goes.

This post may be a simple beginning, but for me, it's the start of a path I've wanted to take for a long time. I'm happy to finally be here.

Until the next post, I'll be out there experimenting, building, learning, and probably breaking a few things along the way.
