## Background

I am a PhD candidate in Tianjin University's **Strong Motion Observation and Simulation Laboratory**, where I investigate soil–structure interaction, seismic wave propagation, and strong ground motion through earthquake-engineering analysis and numerical modeling.

My research centers on the coupled response of soil, foundations, and structures during earthquakes. I am particularly interested in how seismic waves propagate through these systems and how numerical models can improve our understanding of their behavior.

## Current work

My current work investigates the response of soil–foundation–structure systems to earthquake excitation. By combining wave-propagation theory, strong-motion analysis, and numerical simulation, I aim to better characterize the effects of soil and foundation flexibility on structural response.
